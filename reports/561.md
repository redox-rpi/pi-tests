# Test 561 – upstream bump after the TLS fix (rerun of 558)

Date: 2026-11-21. Pi test agent.

## A – Pi 3B+, network boot, `v0.6.0-dev-133-g9b1ce300+x2d42`

Result A: **fail, one check**: the timesync panic is gone, but one warm restart came up without network.

- SHA256SUMS-bump2: all OK. Staged `bump2-3bp-netboot.img` (gzip) + manifest in the TFTP folder for the 3B+.
- `pi-test.py --extended`: **10 of 11 pass**. `version`, `manifest`, `ps`, `ps-tty`, `sdcard`, `audio`, **`time` (set by NTP, Pi clock −1 s)**, `tcp` (3.2 MiB/s), `starts` (0 of 300 failed), `cpu` (1400 MHz, not throttled) all pass.
  **`reboot` fails**: devsh did not answer within 120 s.
  The serial log shows the reason. The Pi reached `redox login:` after the restart, but `dhcpd: eth0` got `no offer` on every attempt (8 rounds of 3). timesync then says `no default route (DHCP not done)`. A serial `reboot` brought the network back.
  Log: [561/a-boot-after-pitest-reboot.log](561/a-boot-after-pitest-reboot.log).
- **20 restarts** over the serial console: **20/20** `redox login:`, 32–35 s each. The network came up every time; in 3 of them (1, 12, 18) dhcpd needed a second attempt.
  `RELIBC PANIC` 0, `UNHANDLED EXCEPTION` 0, `panic` 0 in all 20 boots. (Test 558: timesync panic on 19 of 20.)
  `nothing on the root port`: 2 times (12, 18).
- In total, 1 of 22 boots of this image had no network after a warm restart. In Test 558 (same procedure, before the TLS fix) the `reboot` check passed after 36 s, so a single run cannot tell whether this is new or just rare. Later finding (Test 562): this boot had `device on root port, Full speed` and `enumeration failed: Transfer(130)`, so the LAN7800 was never attached. That is the USB warm-reboot fault (#576), which stable has too.
- Afterwards: stable `v0.6.0-dev-115-gb972ccfd+x2d42` staged again, booted to `redox login:`, devsh answers.
