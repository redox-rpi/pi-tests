- The HDMI side now looks set up from the register view: core, scheduler `000CB028`, FIFO recenter `00004040`, PV2 and HVS running. Yet the NanoKVM sees no signal at all, not even a wrong mode. So it looks like **nothing leaves the PHY**. The PHY words read `00000000 8E000000`. If the first word is a power-down or control register, `00000000` may mean the TMDS lanes or their clock are not enabled. That would be worth comparing with the same registers under start.elf.
- The second `hdmi` stops the frames again (as in row 434: PV2 STAT 444 → 100, VID_CTL bit 19 set). That looks like a missing teardown before the second setup, or an underflow after `HD reset`.
