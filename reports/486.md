### Why the map fails (my reading)

0xfef00700 is the first register block of `hdmi@7ef00700` (the kernel's devicetree list: `0x7ef00700 -> 0xfef00700 len 0x300`). That address is not page-aligned. The DVP reads before it are at the start of a page (`0x7ef00004`, `0x7ef00008`) and work. So the map call probably needs a page-aligned address, with the offset added afterwards. Please check this in socdump (PR #544), not in the kernel.
