# 494: rerun of 486 with the socdump fix (PR #544 7c58ff66): Pi 400 HDMI registers (#543)

**Result: socdump now reads both HDMI ports completely, with no hang and no panic.**
- **Boot A (default config, no screen):** both ports are set to 640×480 timing, but idle. The PHY is held in reset and powered down, no video, 0 frames.
- **Boot B (`hdmi_force_hotplug=1`, `hdmi_drive=2`):** **the firmware drives HDMI0 with no sink.**
  - HDMI mode, video on, PHY out of reset, **25.200 MHz pixel clock, 60 frames in 1 s**.
  - The audio info frame packet is enabled (`ram_packet_config 0x00010015`), and `MAI_FMT 0x80000000`.
  - But MAI is off (`enabled 0`), and **N and CTS are 0**: the firmware sets up no audio clock regeneration.
  - HDMI1 stays idle in B.
- The DVP block is the same in both boots: `sw_init 0x00000000`, `misc_config 0x00000000`.

- Pi 400, network boot, empty SD slot, no HDMI cable, serial reboots only, no person. 2026-11-17, 19:10–19:19 UTC.
- Image `~/redox-rpi-audio/keep/pi400-socdump-stage/`: img.gz `f579f46dbf…`, toml `7c0f859c85…`, both match the row. The banner showed `v0.5.0-dev-113-g956a5692+x54e8` on both boots.
- Commands ran over devsh; all outputs are below in full.

- Boot A shows both ports idle, as expected. They are not reset at the DVP level, but their PHYs are held in reset and video is off.
