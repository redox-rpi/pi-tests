## Boot time (lead's question: does the change lengthen startup?)

Medians, from `kreset` (stable: only the boots with a network, 12 including the recovery boots):

| | P (#729), 31 boots | stable, 12 good boots | Test 568 image D |
|---|---|---|---|
| U-Boot hands over to the loader (`Booting /`) | 20.93 s | 20.92 s | 20.83 s |
| **`redox login:`** | **33.16 s** (32.21–36.07) | **33.25 s** (32.27–35.97) | 35.78 s |
| login after the loader starts | **12.22 s** (12.08–12.77) | **12.31 s** (12.15–12.37) | 14.93 s |
| **`dwc2d: link up`** | **34.47 s** (33.47–38.06) | **34.40 s** (33.48–38.09) | 34.06 s (row: 34.1 s) |
| `DHCP: eth0 got` | 34.67 s | 34.60 s | 35.10 s |
| devsh answers after the `reboot` command | **29 s** (26–38) | **28 s** (26–41) | 29 s (22–56) |

- **P and stable differ by 0.1 s or less on every mark**. That is within the scatter, so the default patch does not lengthen startup.
- The spread of 2–3 s between boots comes from U-Boot (`Booting /` at about 20.0, 20.9 or 23.6 s); it is the same in all three images.
- Link-up is 0.4 s later than in Test 568, but equal to stable. Test 568's image was older (`v0.6.0-dev-125`), and its login came 2.6 s later.
- devsh times have a 1–2 s step, from the polling loop.

Afterwards: stable is staged and running with devsh (08:04, after the third fault boot's serial reset).
