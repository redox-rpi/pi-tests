# 469: Pi 3B+ `poweroff` halts through the watchdog (#418, kstop-halt f72acca2)

**Result: pass** (the Architecture session decided this on 2026-11-16 after reading the points below: the selector banner is expected, because the bootcode reads the halt flag; the missing `poweroff: shutdown through kstop` line is not part of #418; 0.00 W and a silent console stand in for the ping; no rerun). The Pi halts: power 0.0 W, no U-Boot or Redox afterwards. Measured against the row text, two points differ:
- The watchdog reset runs the card's bootcode once. 0.2 s after `kstop: halting through the BCM2835 watchdog`, the boot selector printed its banner and chained Broadcom's `bootcode.bin`, and then everything stayed quiet for 3 minutes. The row wanted no firmware output at all.
- `poweroff: shutdown through kstop` does not show on the serial line, in A or in B.
- The ping check did not run, because the build host has no `ping` (my mistake). The Shelly reading of 0.0 W stands in for it.

Before (A, stable) the Pi kept drawing 4.4 W after `poweroff`. After (B), it draws 0.0 W.

- Pi 3B+, network boot, selector card in, power through the Shelly plug, no person. 2026-11-16, 20:49–21:03 UTC.
- B image: `v0.6.0-dev-25-gf72acca2+x42c6`, built in my worktree from `kstop-halt` f72acca2. `grep -c PM_RSTS_RASPBERRYPI_HALT …/bcm2835_wdt.rs` gives **2**.
- Build note: the first build failed with `Package PackageName("bzip2") not found`. Building `bzip2` alone first (`build-image.sh r.bzip2`) and then the image worked. See the end of this report.
- **Firmware:** the Pi boots our boot selector (`redox-rpi bootcode 233c8c5-bootsel`). Its default boot chains the **Broadcom** `bootcode.bin` (`rbc> chain built-in bootcode.bin, 52624 bytes, crc32 53C9B136`), and that boots U-Boot and Redox.

- **Build note for the script owners:** after `relibc differs from the prefix sysroot's: rebuilding the sysroot and static recipes`, `make static_clean` removes `bzip2`. The image's `curl` needs it, and with `REPO_BINARY=1` the installer then stops with `Package PackageName("bzip2") not found`. `scripts/build-image.sh r.bzip2`, followed by a normal build, fixed it. (Release & CI: this is #519, fixed on main by #521 since v0.6.0-dev-40-g185c4d73. `kstop-halt` at dev-25 does not have the fix; builds from main, or branches with main merged in, do not need the workaround.)
