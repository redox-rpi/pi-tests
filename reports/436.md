Notes from the test agent, unchecked against the code:
- The value that changed is A2W `7E102088`, which lk-overlay sets to `18008E` and our firmware left at `18048E`: bit 10 (`0x400`) differs. If that register is PLLH's ANA2 (or similar), our firmware sets one analogue bit that lk-overlay and Linux do not. The rest of the HDMI path was already fine in part A; this bit decided whether a signal leaves the Pi. Worth checking which write it was, one at a time in a new row, and putting it into `hdmi`.
- All writes used the 0x5A password and went in after PLLH had locked; the picture came without a new `hdmi` or PLLH relock.
