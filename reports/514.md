## The first boot found no USB device

- **Run 1 (08:28):** dwc2d logged `nothing on the root port`, so the hub and the LAN7800 were never found. There was no network and no devsh, and pi-test failed with `No route to host`.
  - irqstat then showed only `89 uart-pl011`, with `source_empty` 0 and the sums correct.
  - I restored stable by power cycle.
- **Run 2 (only a boot plus one read):** the hub was found, with `41 usb-dwc2` 120 and `89` 39 plus `source_empty` 1, which equals line 8 = 160.
- **Run 3:** the full test above.
- So 1 of 3 boots of this image had no USB. I have not seen this with stable. The cause is not known; it may be chance.
