# Test 690: v0.7.0-rc1 card, short person sound check, Pi 3B+

**Result: pass.** Two boots of the v0.7.0-rc1 card (with the `audiod` fix #775 for #770). In both, the startup chime played in full, Sopwith had sound over HDMI, and `tone chime > /scheme/audio` after Sopwith ended in under 2 s.

Pi 3B+, the maintainer at the Pi with an HDMI monitor and speakers, 2026-11-28 09:46–10:02 UTC. The maintainer wrote the card with `sudo swiftflash redox:redox-rpi`, version `v0.7.0-rc1`. Banner of both boots: `redox-rpi v0.7.0-rc1 (v0.7.0-dev-120-g637c7514+x6a9f) (direct, 2026-11-28T09:20:08Z)`.

| Boot | startup chime full | Sopwith sound (~30 s, from the launcher) | `tone chime` after Sopwith (serial console) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | yes (maintainer; `startup-sound: played`) | yes (maintainer) | **1.7 s**, exit 0 |
| 2 (power cycle) | yes (maintainer; `startup-sound: played`) | yes (maintainer) | **1.9 s**, exit 0 |

- I typed the chime on the serial console myself (logged in as root, `echo T0; tone chime > /scheme/audio; echo RC-CHIME $?`). The time runs from the `T0` line to the `RC-CHIME` line in the serial log, read every 0.2 s, so ±0.2 s.
- Extra from the maintainer: the volume keys work (Super plus the up/down/mute keys).
- Serial log, both boots: kernel panic 0, `RELIBC PANIC` 0, UNHANDLED 0. `bcm2835-hdmiaudiod: HDMI vc4 … HDMI mode, pixel clock 148.500 MHz` present.
- Compared with Test 675 (candidate before the fix): there the chime after the desktop did not finish in 4 minutes and Sopwith was almost silent. That does not happen on rc1 here.
