# Test 707: #671 G5 step 4, vc4d switches HDMI0 to 1280x720 itself, Pi 400

**Result: fail by the row, though the checks themselves look wrong.** In both boots vc4d switched to 720p, and two of its own checks failed: `HVS channel counts 240 frames/s, not 60` and `the monitor's SCDC reports no lock (status 0x03)`. vc4d went back to the firmware's mode, and the same two checks failed there too (`restore: FAILED`). Then `taking over channel 1 at 1920x1080`, and the desktop ran at 1920x1080 with GL compositing on the GPU. Panic 0, no hang, no power cycle.

**Why the checks look wrong:** the `before:` line, measured in the firmware's own 1080p mode before any switch, already says `240 HVS frames/s, 60 encoder frames/s`. So 240 is the HVS counter's normal rate here, not a sign of a black screen. The encoder counter shows 60 in all three states. The SCDC line also reads `status 0x03: clock detected, channels locked`, yet the check reports "no lock". Both FAILEDs therefore say nothing about whether 720p was visible. Nobody watched the monitor.
