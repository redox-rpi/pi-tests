How to read it:
- The chime always adds 138 loud chunks, so its samples all arrive. What changes is how many chunks (and so how much time) pass meanwhile: V6 about 200 chunks for the chime, C1 fresh 553, C2 fresh 729 and after the desktop 567. The device keeps running (chunks go on at the same rate, no new underruns or DMA faults), but the chime's data comes in more slowly.
- Sopwith on C2: the device played 2816 chunks in 30 s, only 89 of them loud. So Sopwith's audio mostly did not reach the driver, while the driver kept playing silence. That matches the user's "almost no sound" in Test 675.
- C1 fresh 6.0 s, but 2.0 s after the desktop: one odd reading. It may be a one-off, but it hints that the slower chime already starts before C2.
