# Test 474 – v0.6.0-rc1 automated, Pi 3B+

Date: 2026-11-21, 17:52–18:59 UTC. Pi test agent. Build ID `v0.6.0-dev-125-g5acacd83+xc80e` (commit 5acacd83), payloads from `~/redox-rpi-rel/keep/` (SHA256SUMS-v0.6.0-rc1).

Result: **fail, one finding in the build: curl (and everything that needs libz) does not start**, because `/usr/lib/libz.so.1` is missing from the rc1 images. Release and CI found the cause: zlib was built static only. That is a build fault, to be fixed in an rc2.
All other checks of A, B and C pass.
Two side notes:
- devsh refused connections once after pi-test. That was not reproduced in a second run.
- The USB warm-reboot fault (#576) hit 3 of the reboots. It is a known fault, also on stable, and fixed by #576 v3 (Test 568).

**devsh refused after pi-test (seen once).**
- In the first run, a clock sampler polled devsh every 5 s alongside pi-test, and every one of its calls got no answer from 17:53:40 on.
- When pi-test ended at 17:55:58, devsh refused new connections (`Connection refused`). So steps 2, 3 and 7 failed at once, and reboot 1 could not be sent over devsh. The serial log shows nothing from devshd.
- A serial reboot fixed it. Steps 2, 3 and 7 were then done in a second boot of the same image ([474/a2/](474/a2/)).
- That second boot also tested overlapping devsh sessions (one 20 s session plus three short ones at the same time): all answered, and devsh still accepted afterwards.
- A third boot ran pi-test `--starts 1000` alone, and devsh answered 6/6 times right after it ([474/a4/](474/a4/)).
- So this was not reproduced. It may need many overlapping connections over minutes; a note for the devsh owner.
