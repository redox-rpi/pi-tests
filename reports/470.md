# 470: Boot time of a minimal Redox with our firmware, direct boot on the 3B+

> **Correction (2026-11-17): the `redox login:` times in this report are about 2.2 s too late.** My capture tool (`tsl.py`) stamped a line when its newline arrived. The prompt has no newline, so it got the time of the next console line (`arm: clock 600 MHz set …`, about 2.2 s later). Counted correctly, login comes about 0.03 s after the banner: **about 12.85 s after our firmware's first line** (banner 12.81 / 12.82 / 12.83 s), not 15.09 s; from the Shelly about 20.25 s, not 22.49 s. Kernel to login is then about 7.75 s, not 9.99 s. The 2.3 s from banner to login (rof#44) is this artifact. The pass verdict does not change. `tsl.py` now prints a partial line after 50 ms of quiet, with its own time.

**Result: pass.** All 3 boots reached `redox login:`, with the banner `redox-rpi v0.5.1-dev-101-g000f605e+x9f76 (direct minimal: keyboard, no network, 2026-11-16T03:50:51Z)`. There were no new errors compared with row 468. **Login came 15.1 s after our firmware started** (average of 3, spread 0.05 s), or 22.5 s after the Shelly switched on. In this setup the extra 7.4 s is the boot selector plus sending the payload over serial. Started from the card as `bootcode.bin`, that would be about **15.5 s from power on** (estimate; see below).

- Pi 3B+, selector card in. Payload chained over serial as in rows 467/468, power cycles through the Shelly plug, no person. 2026-11-16, 20:34–20:46 UTC.
- Payload `~/rbc-build/payloads/min-20261116a/`:
  - `bootcode-min-20261116a.bin`: 34 980 bytes, sha256 `1ebf71b4…`, crc32 4F5AEBD1, banner `min-20261116a (release, boots from the SD card, boot screen on HDMI)`.
  - kernel8.img a8d13d40.
  - initramfs 42 278 912 bytes, crc32 5a121d47.
  - redox-3bp.dtb 2522dc16.
  - cmdline.txt fdcffa2f (`RPI_FB=1280x720`).
- **Card setup:**
  - The four files went to `mmc 2:1` from U-Boot (`wget`, `crc32`, `fatwrite`, `fatload`, `crc32` again). Each CRC was the same before and after writing.
  - The bootcode was chained, not written as `bootcode.bin`, because the card's `bootcode.bin` is the boot selector.
  - The row's `config.txt` was not written either; the card keeps its own (the selector's). That `config.txt` differs only in the `boot_title` line, which only sets the boot screen title.

Started from the card, our firmware's first line would come at about that point, so login would be at about 0.4 + 15.1 = **15.5 s from power on**.

### Afterwards

- Restore: power cycle into the selector's default boot and stop the autoboot. Then `fatrm mmc 2:1` for the four files. `fatls` shows 24 files and 3 folders again, the same listing as after row 468. Then `boot` into stable.
- The 3B+ runs stable `v0.5.1-dev-101-g000f605e+x2d42` (netboot) at `redox login:`. `/srv/tftp/redox` is stable. The HTTP copies are removed, and the selector card stays in.
