Result: **fail, one step further than Test 638.**
- Mesa now offers `EGL_EXT_image_dma_buf_import yes, _modifiers yes`, and prime-probe no longer crashes.

- The desktop comes up after the reboot.
- Regressions pass. No crash, no watchdog reset, no kernel panic, no UNHANDLED in any part.

- **What changed from 638:** Mesa advertises dma-buf import now, and the failure path ends cleanly with a FAIL line instead of jumping to a bad address.
- **Where it stops:** `eglCreateImageKHR` with `EGL_LINUX_DMA_BUF_EXT` returns no image. errno is EBADF, while the EGL error is `EGL_SUCCESS`. Most likely the dma-buf file descriptor that prime-probe gets for vc4d's scanout buffer is not one that Mesa can use, for example `fstat`/`lseek`/`mmap` on it fails with EBADF.
- **Serial:** neither vc4d nor v3dd logged an error or a line about an export or import. vc4d logged only its screenshots. In part A2 it also logged `vblank: 60 interrupts; flips shown on the interrupt 56, on the timer 2`.
- **The reboot before step 5 works:** after it, `desktop --yes` and the login bring the desktop up, as Graphics expected.
