- The endless-job check frees its BO while the job still runs. With the queue, v3dd now answers `EBUSY` instead of keeping the BO until the job is done, so the probe's old check fails. This is the behaviour the row describes as new ("a BO freed while its jobs run stays until they are done"); either v3dd should accept the free and defer it, or the old `--faults` check needs updating.
- After step 3, `live_bos 1` (that BO). By the end of step 4, `live_bos 0`, so it was freed later.
- The `mapped:` line says `PASS: both ways, and after a free while mapped`, not the expected `mapped: no BOs leaked`.
- Serial: the expected `v3dd: timeout: bin job (1000 ms)` and `reset: done, MMU set up again` lines for the endless jobs; `MMU_VIO_ADDR` is still `0x0`.
- `resets after` each step: 0 (no U-Boot line, no watchdog reset). Panic 0, UNHANDLED 0. devsh answered to the end.
