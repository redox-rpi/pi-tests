Everything else passes:

### Notes for the decision

- **In A1 and A3, login appears at the moment the link comes up**, not earlier (6.73 vs 6.73, 6.69 vs 6.69). In A2 it came 1.4 s before the link (5.59 vs 6.97). Login may still wait on something that the first frames or the link unblock, in 2 of 3 boots. Without that, the gain would be about 3 s, as in A2.
- **From power on:**
  - B login 35.5 / 35.8 / 35.7 s; A 33.3 / 33.5 / 33.6 s; A − B = −2.2 s (median).
  - But the kernel started at 26.9 / 26.9 / 27.1 s in B and 27.7 / 27.8 / 27.7 s in A. A was already 0.85 s later at U-Boot's `redox-rpi: boot net redox/` (B2 16.2 s, A2 17.0 s after power on), before the image is loaded.
  - Loading the image and starting the kernel took the same time in both (6.56 s from the last TFTP transfer to the first `kernel::` line). So that 0.8 s is noise in the firmware/U-Boot part, and the row's kernel-relative base is the fair one.
