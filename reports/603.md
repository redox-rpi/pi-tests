What the numbers suggest (for the graphics session to check):
- The interrupt fires for about half of the first 100 compute jobs, then hardly at all: 16 interrupts for the 1000 jobs of step 4, and 32 for the 100 render jobs. Maybe the interrupt is not re-enabled or acknowledged after the first few, or the job ends before v3dd waits.
- **The fallback timer is much faster than 5 ms.** During the 1 s endless job in step 3, `timer_wakeups` grew by about 8500, so one wake-up about every 0.12 ms. A 5 ms timer would give about 200. So the fallback works like a fast poll. That is also why the jobs are still fast (4–8 us average, as before).
- The jobs are no slower than in Tests 594/601 (compute 4–8 us average; render 63 us average, faster than 594's 116 us).
