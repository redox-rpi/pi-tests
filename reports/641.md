## v3dd lines around each prime-probe run

The same three lines in every run (A shown; A2 is the same; B has 8294400 bytes / 2025 pages and BO 4 at `0xce0000`):

```
v3dd: imported a buffer of 1228800 bytes as BO 1 at V3D 0x100000 (300 pages, scattered)
v3dd: imported a buffer of 1228800 bytes as BO 2 at V3D 0x102000 (300 pages, scattered)
v3dd: imported a buffer of 1228800 bytes as BO 4 at V3D 0x2ce000 (300 pages, scattered)
```

- **BO 1 covers `0x100000`–`0x22c000`, and BO 2 starts at `0x102000`, inside BO 1.** At 1080p BO 1 covers `0x100000`–`0x8e9000`, and BO 2 again starts at `0x102000`.
- So the second import seems to get an address only 2 pages after the first, as if the address allocator reserved 2 pages for an imported buffer instead of its size. What the GPU writes through one buffer then lands partly in the other. That would explain the leftover pattern and wrong corners while the centre is right.
- BO 4 (a third import, probably the probe's own or the foreign buffer) is placed after the end of BO 1.
- No v3dd error, timeout or fault in any part. vc4d logged only its screenshots.
