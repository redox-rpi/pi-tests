- Under our firmware the same 8–10 `call_fdread` lines come, and then
  `rpi-firmwared` never prints its first line.
- Our firmware prints one `mbox: <tag>` line for every request it serves.
  It printed them for U-Boot (the last one before the kernel, at U-Boot's
  EFI step), but **none after the kernel started**.
- So the hang is very likely `rpi-firmwared`'s first mailbox call. Either
  its request never reaches the VPU, or the answer never comes back. That
  is my reading of the log, not a measurement.
