## Part 2 – LAN and NTP (Test 449 steps 2 and 3)

Date: 2026-11-22, afternoon UTC. The user did it, and the coordinator passed the results on. It ran on a second Zero 2 W with the same rc3 card, using a PoE HAT with a USB hub and Ethernet (USB Realtek adapter); the 3B+'s network cable was borrowed for it. This Zero has no serial logger, so there is no serial log.

Result: **pass** (as the user reports).

- **Step 2 (REQ-NET-2 crit 1, REQ-BOARD-1 crit 2):** after boot, `ifconfig` on the console shows an address from DHCP (`<lan-ip>`) ✔. Timing, by hand, on a second boot of this Zero: about 10 s from the end of the firmware's colour screen to the DHCP address. The login prompt comes before the address. The firmware phase (loading the ~256 MiB initramfs, about 12 s by Architect's estimate in #615) was not timed. So power-on to address is roughly 20–25 s, well under 60 s, but hand-timed and with no serial log. The HAT's USB Ethernet chip is not known yet (it was called Realtek).
- **Step 3 (REQ-TIME-1):** `date` shows the right time in UTC (NTP) ✔.
