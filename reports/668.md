## The hub problem from boot ([usb-lines.log](668/usb-lines.log))

1. 00:36.1–36.3: the keyboard's `SET_INTERFACE 0/1 refused` (a stall, completion 6). This is known and harmless in earlier tests.
2. **00:41.358 `port 1.4: CUSTOM_DEVICE_REQ timed out after 5 s, stopping endpoint 0`**, then `STOP_ENDPOINT … failed … status 13000000` and **`endpoint 0 not stopped (I/O error), resetting it`**.
3. **From 00:46.5: `port 1: CUSTOM_DEVICE_REQ timed out after 5 s` about every 7.3 s** (the hub, slot 1). Each is followed by `no event for the stopped CUSTOM_DEVICE_REQ` and usbhubd's `port 3: status not read: Connection timed out` / `port 4: … I/O error`. Hub control transfers end with completion `1B` (27).
4. This runs through the whole test: 35 timeouts in all. The No Op commands still ran clean in the middle of it.
5. With the hub's EP0 broken, the power trigger in step 3 only half works (one mouse re-attach in round 1), and usbhubd finally aborts on a timed-out SET_FEATURE(PORT_POWER).

**My reading:** the ring fix works. The new step at boot is the timed-out request on the keyboard's endpoint 0 after its stalled SET_INTERFACE, which is then reset by the new code. After that the hub's endpoint 0 never answers again. This may come from 0024's reset path or from 0029.
