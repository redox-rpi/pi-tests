## For the audio session

- The sleep after Sopwith ends works. Step 4 only needs Sopwith to really end.
- SIGTERM is ignored. SDL turns SIGTERM into an `SDL_QUIT` event, and Sopwith may not quit on it, or the signal may not reach Sopwith on Redox. Worth checking SIGINT too, and what row 335 used.
- The one underrun is from boot (the chime). Sopwith added none.
