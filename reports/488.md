### Switching from A to B, and what I learned

Writing the B `config.txt` needed a U-Boot prompt with a working serial input. With the A `config.txt`, every normal start goes straight to Redox. Three ways did not work:

1. **Row 468's U-Boot payload** (`bootcode-rlrel-sd34-uboot`) reads the card's `config.txt` too. It loaded the initramfs and stopped with `err fdt: no 4- or 8-byte /chosen linux,initrd-start`, before U-Boot.
2. **Row 476's monitor build** with `boot64 u-boot.bin bcm2710-rpi-3-b-plus.dtb`: U-Boot started, but without overlays its console is on the mini UART, which the VPU firmware also uses. Typed characters did not reach U-Boot.
3. **GPIO3 driven low in the selector's monitor** (`w 7e200028 8`, `w 7e200000 200`; GPLEV0 bit 3 read 0), then the normal boot: Broadcom's firmware still took the direct path. It must reset the pin or read it differently.

**What worked:** GPIO3 driven low in the selector's monitor, then Test 484's firmware build `bootcode-cfg-20261117b.bin` chained.
- It reads `config.txt` and honours `[gpio3=0]`: `boot64: config.txt: kernel u-boot.bin, initramfs -, … 2 overlays`.
- It applies `disable-bt` and `mmc`, and starts U-Boot with its console on the PL011, where typing works.

This is a way to reach a U-Boot prompt remotely on a direct card. Our firmware evaluates `[gpio3=0]` from the GPIO level the monitor set, which Broadcom's firmware does not.
