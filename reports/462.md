### My reading (not checked further)

- During a **failed TFTP try**, lwIP's UDP layer takes in 47–406 datagrams per try (21 TFTP intervals; IP recv about equal to UDP recv) and drops at most 2, yet TFTP still ends with `Loading: Timeout!`. UDP recv counts every datagram accepted at the UDP layer, broadcasts included, so these needn't be TFTP data. But the frames aren't lost in the driver or in lwIP's lower layers.
- During a **failed HTTP try**, TCP sometimes receives almost nothing (as little as +1 segment; e.g. boot 1 try 3: TCP recv +4, xmit +11) and sometimes hundreds of segments before `HTTP client error 5` (boot 1 try 2: TCP recv +493).
- **ETHARP proterr** (frames of a type lwIP doesn't handle) is a third or more of all received frames in every interval. Probably the LAN's non-IPv4 traffic (IPv6 and others). It also happens in good intervals.
- UMAC_CMD 0x8 in every dump means the MAC is stopped when `redox_netdiag` runs. The registers can't show the state during the failed transfer, only afterwards.
