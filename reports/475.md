# 475: Our bootcode stays off after a halt (PM_RSTS partition 63, rof #40, branch `halt-40`), Pi 3B+

**Result: pass on boot and halt; the power comparison cannot tell the two states apart.**
- A boots normally to `redox login:` with no `halt:` line.
- B prints `halt: the OS halted the Pi (PM_RSTS 00000555, partition 63); staying off, GPIO3 (pin 5) to ground wakes it` and then nothing for 200 s.
- The B reading is **0.00 W** (all 30 samples, current 0.000 A). But the idle selector in the monitor **also reads 0.00 W**, so "clearly below the idle selector" cannot be shown with this plug. It measures nothing under roughly 1 W; both states are below that.

**On the power readings:**
- The Shelly Plug reports 0.0 W for both the idle selector (Boot ROM plus our bootcode in its monitor loop, SDRAM up) and the halted Pi.
- Running Redox reads 2.8–3.1 W; with Broadcom's firmware halted (row 469 B), it is also 0.00 W.
- So "does it reach 0.0 W" is **yes**. But this plug cannot rank the halted state against the idle selector. That needs a USB power meter in the Pi's supply, which is a person task.
