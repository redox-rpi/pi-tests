**Notes**
- One `err` line: `err expander: no FXL6408 at 43 (id 00)`. The GPIO expander exists only on the 3B+; on the Zero this is expected. The firmware could skip the probe on boards without it (cosmetic).
- `[ERROR redoxfs] failed to open filesystem /scheme/disk.mmc/0` (and `0p0`): the direct card runs from the live disk in RAM, so this is the usual line, not a fault.
- Timings: Boot ROM to our first line not measurable here (Boot ROM timer 1005085 us at our start); our part up to the ARM 23.5 s, of which 22.5 s is the initramfs; Redox up to login 7.4 s.
- Earlier the same day the user tried `rpi-open-firmware-v0.1.0-rc1-pi3bp.img.xz` on the Zero: a stream of `8` lines (log `serial-zero2w-20261118-175242.log`). That card is for the 3B+ only (1 GB SDRAM set-up, `redox-3bp.dtb`), so that is expected and no finding.
