# 497: where does the GPIO3 wake come from? The selector drives GPIO3 low itself (rof #40 / PR #52)

**Result: the poll works as intended.**
- At 30 s into the halt, `driving GPIO3 low now` came, then 41 ms later `halt: GPIO3 low, starting again`.
- 218 ms after that came a new banner (the card's 56354b8) with Boot ROM **`timer 210121 us`**, the watchdog-reset value, not a power-on.
- There was **no second `halt:` line**, and the boot went on normally to stable `redox login:` (35 s after the wake).

So, as the firmware session thought, Test 495's wake was a power-on reset from the wire, not this path.

- Pi 3B+, selector card with 56354b8, nothing written to the card, no person. 2026-11-17, 20:03–20:06 UTC.
- Payload `~/rbc-build/payloads/sel-20261117a/bootcode-selector-haltdiag-20261117c.bin`: 61636 bytes, sha256 `5836f37f2344…` (matches the row), crc32 `D21C4EA4`.
- Steps as in Test 496:
  - Shelly power cycle.
  - Cold start with Enter (`ok monitor` from 56354b8).
  - Load, then `w 7e100020 5a000555`, then `chain`.
  - Serial captured with times for 120 s.
