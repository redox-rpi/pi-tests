# Test 562 – DHCP after a warm reboot on the Pi 3B+ (bump)

Date: 2026-11-21. Pi test agent. Image `v0.6.0-dev-133-g9b1ce300+x9f05` (`~/zrtl-q/warm-3bp-stage/`, both sha256 as in the row).

Result: **fail, 22/30** boots had devsh within 120 s. The cause is USB, not DHCP: in all 8 failed boots dwc2d found **nothing on the root port**.

## Counts

- 30 warm reboots, each `reboot` over devsh, then waiting up to 120 s for devsh.
  - **22 ok**: devsh after 27–41 s (counted from the `reboot` command); `no offer` 0 every time.
  - **8 fail** (boots 8, 14, 15, 19, 21, 22, 23, 29).
- `RELIBC PANIC` 0 and `UNHANDLED EXCEPTION` 0 in every boot.
- Timings for the good boots, from the timestamped serial capture ([serial-timestamped.log](562/serial-timestamped.log)), measured from `kreset`:
  - `dwc2d: link up`: 33.2–36.8 s (median 33.3 s);
  - `DHCP: eth0 got`: 0.8 s after link up (34.0–37.6 s);
  - `redox login:`: 34.6–38.3 s.

## The failed boots

Every one of the 8 has the same pattern ([fail-boot8.log](562/fail-boot8.log), the others as `fail-bootN.log`):

- U-Boot sees `4 USB Device(s)` (hub + LAN7800) before Redox starts.
- Redox dwc2d then logs `dwc2d: nothing on the root port` and `devices ready after 3060 ms`.
- There is no hub, no LAN7800 and no `dwc2d: link up` line.
- dhcpd sends anyway, and gets `no offer` 9 times by the time netdiag runs.
- netdiag over serial ([fail-boot8-netdiag.txt](562/fail-boot8-netdiag.txt) …) shows the same each time: `network.usb/adapters 0`, `rx_frames 0`, `tx_errors 0`, `eth0/addr/list Not configured`, `irq/98 23`.

So no frame ever arrives, because the adapter was never enumerated. A good boot is [good-boot9.log](562/good-boot9.log) (`device on root port, High speed`, hub 0424:2514, …).

Recovery: a `reboot` on the serial console brought the network back 7 times. After boot 21 the serial reboot also gave `nothing on the root port`, so I power-cycled the Pi with the plug (allowed for the 3B+).

## Same on stable

When I staged stable `v0.6.0-dev-115-gb972ccfd+x2d42` again and rebooted over devsh, that boot also had `nothing on the root port` and no network. A serial reboot fixed it. So this is **not new in the bump**; the warm reboot leaves the root port empty about 1 time in 4 (9 of the 39 warm resets in this row).

## Test 561 A was a different case

The failed boot in Test 561 A was not this one.

- There, dwc2d saw `device on root port, Full speed` (it should be High speed).
- `GET_DESCRIPTOR` to address 0 then failed with `Transfer(130)` three times, and dwc2d gave up with `enumeration failed: Transfer(130)`.
- The result was the same: no adapter, `no offer`.

So there are two warm-reboot failure modes:
1. The root port is empty.
2. The port comes up at Full speed and enumeration fails.

Afterwards: stable staged, booted with a serial reboot to `redox login:`, devsh answers.
