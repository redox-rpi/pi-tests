# 518: Pi 400 HDMI sound under desktop load (#543, PRs #548, #552, #567)

**Result: pass. But the desktop ran at 640x480, not 1080p, so the "pixel clock follows a mode change" part was not exercised.**

- No panic, no hang.

  - A 1080p mode needs `hdmi_group`/`hdmi_mode` in `config.txt` too, or a monitor (Test 501 had 1080p).
