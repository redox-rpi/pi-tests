# Test 564 – empty USB root port after a warm reboot: A/B with a root port power cycle (#576)

Date: 2026-11-21, 11:38–14:38 UTC. Pi test agent. Both stages pass `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
A `v0.6.0-dev-122-gae8fa385+x42c6` (without), B `v0.6.0-dev-122-gae8fa385+xa91e` (with `usb-port-power`).

Result: **fail.**
- **A shows the fault** in 12 of 30 boots.
- **B does not fix it**: the hub was missing in 14 of 30 boots. Each time, the two port power cycles left the port as empty as before.
- No `RELIBC PANIC` and no `UNHANDLED EXCEPTION` in any boot.

## Network outage

The user's network update fell into the start of this row.
- The staging boot of A (11:39:21) got no devsh, and the Shelly did not answer 11:40:57–11:44:30.
- The coordinator saw everything working again at 11:52.
- The staging boot was recovered with a power cycle at 11:52:27. A's first counted reboot started after 11:53:29, so **no counted reboot falls in the outage window** and none was added.
- My gateway check used `ping`, which the host does not have; its `gw=DOWN` lines before 11:53 are not valid ([netwatch.txt](564/netwatch.txt)).

## The register lines (B)

Every empty boot in B logs exactly these three lines, always with the same values:

```
dwc2d: root port empty: Timeout("device connect"), HPRT0 0x1000 GUSBCFG 0x20001400 GOTGCTL 0x1c0000; port power off and on (1/2)
dwc2d: root port empty: Timeout("device connect"), HPRT0 0x1000 GUSBCFG 0x20001400 GOTGCTL 0x1c0000; port power off and on (2/2)
dwc2d: nothing on the root port, HPRT0 0x1000 GUSBCFG 0x20001400 GOTGCTL 0x1c0000
```

What the values mean:
- `HPRT0 0x1000`: only port power (bit 12) is on. Connect status, enable and line state are all 0.
- No `root port failed` line appeared.
- A's line has no registers (`dwc2d: nothing on the root port`).

The dwc2d lines of every failed boot are in `564/{A,B}-fail-bootN-dwc2d.txt`, and the full timestamped serial log is [564/serial-timestamped.log](564/serial-timestamped.log).

## Recovery and cold boots

The recovery was a serial `reboot`, and a second one if needed. It often needed two tries; the first serial reboot after a failed boot was empty again in most cases.

Power cycles with the plug were needed at 12:43 (A), 13:32 (B) and 14:24 (B).
- After the 12:43 power cycle (a cold boot), dwc2d also found **nothing on the root port**.
- The 13:32 cold boot found the hub.

So the fault is not limited to warm reboots.

Afterwards: stable `v0.6.0-dev-115-gb972ccfd+x2d42` staged, booted to `redox login:`, devsh up (14:37:50).
