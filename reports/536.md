# 536: Pi 400 with all 4 GiB, framebuffer and a 200 MiB TCP run, without (A) and with (B) the DMA zone (#299, PR #580)

**Result:**
- **The framebuffer works with 4 GiB, in A and B:**
  - `rpi-fbd: 640x480 stride 640 at 0x3e857000, double buffered`, blitting with the CPU (`RPI_FB_DMA` off);
  - `fbshot` gives `640x480, top colour 98%, 2 colours`, which is the text console.
- **The 200 MiB TCP transfer has the wrong SHA-256 every time, but that is not about memory.** A control run with `total_mem=1024` fails the same way. All 6 runs differ, at 15.5–15.9 MiB/s:

  | Runs | Result |
  |---|---|
  | A and B with 4 GiB | 2 of 2 differ |
  | A with 1 GiB | 2 of 2 differ |
  | A with 4 GiB, again | 2 of 2 differ |

- No panic and no `UNHANDLED`.
- **B** prints `DMA zone: 65536 KiB below 0x40000000`, and `free` shows about 64 MB more used than A.
- So there is still no difference between A and B.

**About the TCP failure:**
- The check (`pi-test.py`/`regression-set.py`, `check_tcp`) receives with `nc <host>:<port> > /tmp/pi-test-tcp.bin` and hashes that file.
- 10 MiB passed in Test 535, and 100 MiB passed in Test 520 (regression-set, the same `check_tcp`), both at about 3.6–7.6 MiB/s.
- 200 MiB now "arrives" at 15.6 MiB/s, twice as fast. That suggests part of the data is missing, not that bytes are changed: `nc` stops early, or the write to `/tmp` is cut short.
- I did not measure the received size. That needs a run with `wc -c`. I am reporting this to the network session.

- Pi 400, network boot from `/srv/tftp/pi400/`, no person, no power cycle. Boots came from `reboot` on the console, or `boot` typed at U-Boot after a failed TFTP load (#394, 1–2 times per boot). 2026-11-20, 11:57–12:18 UTC.
- Payloads unchanged from Test 535:
  - A `keep/genet-4g-stage/`, `v0.6.0-dev-129-g6104c383+x6f4b`;
  - B `keep/genet-4g-mem-stage/`, `v0.6.0-dev-129-g6104c383+x6a0a`.
- **config.txt:** `total_mem=1024` removed, `hdmi_force_hotplug=1` and `hdmi_drive=2` added at the end. Afterwards it was restored (`diff` same).
