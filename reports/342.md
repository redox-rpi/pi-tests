- **A, main + `usb-hub-drop`:** 3 of 3 downloads complete, with the right SHA-256 and no stall.

In B's stall, dwc2d's main thread sat in state `S` (sleeping) with no CPU

events` line. inputd was blocked with 0 CPU, which is normal for it.

**Reading.** With all of dwc2d's sleeps replaced by a yield loop, three long
downloads in a row ran through. Without that, dwc2d's main thread
stops in a sleep that does not end, with its CPU time frozen. netstack then
waits for good in its read from the device, which is the 326 picture. That
points at `nanosleep` (the kernel's timer wake-up, or dwc2d's use of it)
rather than at the USB hardware or inputd.
