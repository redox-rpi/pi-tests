Card: candidate 2, `v0.7.0-dev-95-g7f4dd1e1+x6a9f` (direct), written by the user. Zero part done by the user on 2026-11-27, result relayed by the lead at the user's request; serial log checked here.

## Zero 2 W, #654

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| (a) no USB network adapter | **pass.** Serial log of the Zero, 3 boots of this card, **0 `dhcpd`/DHCP lines in each**. Boot 1: hub 05e3:0610 with only a keyboard (04d9:0006), no adapter, devices ready after 1554 ms. Boots 2 and 3: `dwc2d: nothing on the root port` (HPRT0 0x1000), so nothing on USB at all, devices ready after 7678 ms. Each boot reaches `redox login:` and `startup-sound: played` / HDMI audio. |
| (b) adapter, no cable | **pass** (seen on screen, not on serial). On a second Zero with HDMI and keyboard and a working hub: keyboard at address 3, RTL8152 at address 4 (0bda:8152, chip version 4c10), `RTL8152 no link yet`, devices ready after 845 ms. 0 dhcpd lines to the end of the boot. `ifconfig`: eth0 "Not configured". |
| Cable plugged in afterwards | **pass.** Shortly after: one DHCP line with address and default route, then timesync; `ifconfig` shows eth0 with an address. |

**Deviation from the row:** case (b) ran on a second Zero without a serial console, so there is no serial log for (b) and the lease, only the user's screen photo (the lead has the path).

**Side note:** the first hub the user tried on the Zero was not detected (`root port empty`, HPRT0 0x1000 after a port reset and a power cycle of the port). Boots 2 and 3 above are that hub. Most likely the hub or its cable, not the image: the same card saw a hub in boot 1 and in case (b).
