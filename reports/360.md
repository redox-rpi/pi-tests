- **What the registers say:**
  - **ESR 0xBF000002** is an SError: an asynchronous external abort,
    typically a bus error from a device access.
  - **SPSR 0x80000000** means it came from EL0, a user process.
  - The process has PCIe values in its registers, so I take it to be
    `pcid`. But it printed none of its expected lines (`pcid: BCM2711 PCIe
    at 0xfd500000 …`), so the abort came on its first accesses, before
    any log line.
- **The row's lines:** there is no `pcid`, `PCI`, `rpi-firmwared: VL805`,
  `xhci` or `usb` line from Redox at all. The only such lines are U-Boot's
  (above) and the kernel's devicetree listing.
- **A runner mistake on my side:** my reboot helper took the old `redox
  login:` prompt as the new boot's login. So the `ls /scheme`, `ls
  /scheme/pci` and `cat /scheme/sys/log | grep -i -e xhci` commands went to
  the stopped console and got no answer. I have fixed the helper for the
