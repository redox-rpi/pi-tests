## Known limits at release

- The text editor cannot save ([#33](https://github.com/redox-rpi/redox-rpi/issues/33)).
- `ifconfig` shows no MAC address.
- Serial input loses a key now and then (19 of 20, [#28](https://github.com/redox-rpi/redox-rpi/issues/28)).
- A small click from the audio jack at power-off and reboot (row 286).
- Raw logs are on homenodellm under `~/pitest-tools/t285/`, `t292/`, `trel040/`.
